Meet Chef Reinhard

My journey in pastry began at an early age in the kitchen of my family's restaurant, where freshly baked Austrian pastries were part of everyday life. Surrounded by classics such as Apple Strudel, Sacher Cake, and Gugelhupf, I developed an early interest in the art of baking.

Guided and inspired by internationally experienced professionals, I have built a career spanning Europe, the Middle East, and Asia, refining both my technical skills and creativity along the way.

Today, besides my dedication to the art of pastry, I am passionate about mentoring the next generation of professionals, sharing the knowledge and values that have shaped my own journey.
