My Gratitude

Words from the mentors and colleagues who shaped my journey.

Reflecting on more than two decades in the pastry profession across different countries, I am deeply grateful to the colleagues and mentors who have helped shape both my career and passion for this craft.

I look back with pride on every workplace and the valuable lessons each experience provided, whether in technical expertise, team development, leadership, inspiration, or lasting friendships.

As Hannes Lubinger aptly said, “Learn from the best” or “Unite the Masters.” This philosophy has guided me throughout my journey and continues to do so today.

My journey is far from over. As I move forward, I am committed to passing these values, skills, and experiences on to the next generation of pastry chefs and bakers.
