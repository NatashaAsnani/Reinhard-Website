About Chef Reinhard

I don’t blindly follow recipes.

When creating new desserts and cakes, I draw on technical expertise, ingredient knowledge, and creativity to develop original creations.

This approach has taken me through hotels, resorts and large-scale production kitchens across Europe, the Middle East and Asia.

Signature Creation

My Sacher Cake

My version of the renowned Sacher Cake combines a moist chocolate sponge with apricot confit, enhanced by notes of passion fruit and vanilla. Layers of luxurious 65% dark chocolate mousse and a glossy dark chocolate glaze bring depth and richness, creating an exceptional flavor pairing that elevates this classic dessert.

  1. Dark chocolate glaze glossy finish
  2. Dark chocolate mousse 65% dark chocolate
  3. Apricot confit passion fruit and vanilla
  4. Chocolate sponge moist
